Skill Development

We believe that this is the best skill development program for any child of any level. There are 3 levels to the skill program that have measurable stages that you will need to pass. The beginner, Intermidiate and Elite level.

Atheleticism

We’re known for transforming athleticism through our programs. This program is not only proven to transform your child's vertical jump, speed, agility and quickness, it also teaches you how to directly apply it in-games. The way this program blends on-field skill with athleticism development is truly revolutionary! The most beautiful thing about this program is that we do most of your child's athleticism development work on the field, intertwined with their skill work, which makes their training day extremely efficient and helps with durability and longevity by avoiding overtraining.

Strength

This program is built to make your child more physical on the field and is formatted to increase his/her durability and longevity. Oh did we mention that we have an entire portion for optimizing their conditioning? We incorporate a year round periodized conditioning program to get your child into the best shape of their life so they can express their skills when it matters, without fatigue being an issue.

Mindset

We’ve helped thousands of children chase their dream of playing at the highest level, but this process takes high levels of dedication, persistence, self motivation and level headedness. Erick will be their mentor by providing motivational talks to keep your child on track, advice to balance school and our program, productivity and study tips and mostly importantly: help your child become the best young man/woman that they can possibly be.

Nutrition/Recovery/Lifestyle development

In addition to locking in on their nutrition, we have resources to teach your child how to recover optimally day to day, to improve their lifestyle and to make the most out of their career.
